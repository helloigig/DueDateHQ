# DueDateHQ — Working Records

Three documents. Here's how to use them.

---

## 📘 [01 — Product Brief](./01-product-brief.md)

**What it is:** Everything from the CEO's brief + business plan + value canvas, consolidated and re-organized. Plus the small-firm persona expansion and the reframing arguments we discussed.

**When to read it:** Reference material. Open when you're about to make a design or scoping decision and need to check what the product actually is.

**What's in it:**
- Product thesis (with weaker framings to avoid)
- 4 personas (Sarah · David · Jennifer · + Small Firm)
- 8 pain points, ranked by severity
- Competitor landscape (Excel / Calendar / Karbon / File In Time)
- 3 user stories (with acceptance criteria)
- Value Proposition Canvas (condensed)
- 9 AI leverage points (the business-model argument)
- Business model (pricing, unit economics, ARR scenarios)
- Market sizing (TAM → SAM breakdown)
- Why now (3 structural forces)
- Risks and responses

---

## 🎤 [02 — User Research Playbook](./02-user-research-playbook.md)

**What it is:** Everything you need to do user research today. Mindset, vocabulary, where to find CPAs, what to say, what to ask, what to do with what you learn.

**When to read it:** Right now. This is today's work.

**What's in it:**
- Why primary research matters (1 min read)
- Ground rules (5 rules that matter more than any question)
- Target user profile (who counts, who doesn't)
- Vocabulary cheat sheet (20 terms you need to recognize)
- 5 outreach channels (ranked by speed)
- 5 copy-paste message templates
- LinkedIn targeting (green flags vs red flags)
- Interview question bank (organized by arc)
- Post-call synthesis routine
- Today's action checklist (2-hour blitz)

---

## 🗓 [03 — 10-Day Execution Plan](./03-10-day-execution-plan.md)

**What it is:** The remapped timeline. Day-by-day breakdown with daily tasks, checkpoints, and what to cut if you fall behind.

**When to read it:** Once, in full, to understand the shape. Then daily to check what's on today.

**What's in it:**
- Overview of the 10 days (3 phases: research, build, ship)
- Day-by-day breakdown with concrete tasks and timeboxes
- Critical checkpoints (the "if you miss this, stop and fix it" moments)
- Scope cuts if you fall behind
- Presentation prep guide for Day 10

---

## Quick reference

- **Today:** Open **02-user-research-playbook.md** and run the 2-hour outreach blitz
- **Tomorrow + Wed:** Interviews. Refer back to the question bank in 02.
- **Thu onwards:** Switch to **03-10-day-execution-plan.md** for daily build tasks
- **Any time you forget what the product is:** Open **01-product-brief.md**
