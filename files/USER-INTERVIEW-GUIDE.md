# DueDateHQ — User Interview Guide

> Single-page runbook for conducting 20-minute CPA discovery calls. Open this 10 minutes before each interview. Print page 2–3 for the call itself.

**Companion:** a deeper research playbook with full outreach templates + synthesis routines lives at `_archive/bootcamp-artifacts/02-user-research-playbook.md` — reference it if you need more breadth.

---

## 1 · What these interviews should validate (or kill)

Three strategic bets. Each interview should move at least one of these meters.

| # | Bet | Validates if you hear… | Kills if you hear… |
|---|-----|------------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Weekly triage is a real pain** (Story 1) | "Monday morning is 30–60 min of switching between Excel and Outlook"; spontaneous frustration with the current setup | "Honestly it's fine, I have a system that works" (from multiple CPAs) |
| 2 | **State announcements are under-served** (Story 3) | A specific story: found out about an extension late, from a client or group chat, after a deadline; 6+ clients affected | "My state's DOR emails me when anything changes"; "I haven't had that problem" |
| 3 | **Migration friction is the conversion blocker** (Story 2) | "I tried [Karbon/TaxDome/Canopy] and gave up during import"; they show you their Excel sheet with pride/shame | "Setup was fine"; or they never tried anything (weaker signal) |

**The 4th thing to listen for:** unexpected pain you didn't predict. Write it down verbatim. This is where tier-1 insight lives.

## 2 · Who counts (screen before booking)

### Yes
- Solo CPA / EA owning their own practice
- Small firm (2–5 partners), partner/owner level
- 20+ clients, multi-state work
- Title: Owner · Founder · Principal · Managing Partner

### No
- Big 4 or mid-tier national firm staff (Deloitte, PwC, RSM, BDO, etc.)
- Corporate in-house accountants
- Pure bookkeepers with no tax work
- Staff/senior accountants at larger firms
- CPA exam candidates, students

### Pre-call check (2 min)
Google their firm name. Look at LinkedIn. Confirm they own it and do tax work. If ambiguous, ask on the call first — redirect if wrong fit.

## 4 · The 20-minute structure

Keep this open on your second screen during the call. Don't recite minutes — use them as a safety net.

| Min | Phase | What happens |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| 0–2 | Rapport + setup | 2-sentence intro, ask permission to record, confirm their role |
| 2–5 | Warm-up | "Walk me through last Monday morning." Let them talk. |
| 5–11 | Story 1 — triage | How they plan the week. Tools. The 45-minute problem. |
| 11–16 | Story 3 — state events | The last extension story. How they found out. Who was affected. |
| 16–18 | Story 2 — tool history | What they've tried. Why they stopped. |
| 18–19 | Wildcards | "What else have I not asked about that matters?" |
| 19–20 | Close | Thanks + "who else should I talk to?" |

### Opening script (verbatim)
> Hi [name], thanks so much for the time. Quick context — I'm researching a compliance-intel tool for solo and small-firm CPAs handling multi-state work. I'm not selling anything today; I'm trying to understand how you actually work before building anything. Mind if I record so I can go back to your exact words later? I won't share the recording.
>
> To start simple — walk me through last Monday morning. From when you opened your laptop until you stopped for lunch.

### Closing script
> That was really helpful. Two last things. First — is there anyone else I should be talking to? Other solo CPAs or small-firm partners who'd have interesting perspective?
>
> And second — can I come back to you in 4–6 weeks with what I've learned? Happy to show early designs if you're curious.

## 5 · Question bank (by arc)

Use the highlighted ones (✨) if you only have time for one per section.

### Warm-up — the recent real moment
- ✨ Walk me through last Monday morning — from opening your laptop until lunch.
- How many clients are you currently serving? Roughly how many states?
- What did the last two weeks of filing season look like?
- What was the single longest task in your week that you wish was faster?

### Story 1 — weekly triage pain
- ✨ Tell me about the last time you *almost* missed a deadline. What actually went wrong?
- How do you decide what to work on first each morning?
- Can you show me your current setup? (Screen-share if possible — gold.)
- If I asked you right now "what's due this week across all your clients," how long would it take you to answer confidently?
- When was the last time you double-checked your own tracking system because you weren't sure?

### Story 3 — state authority announcements
- ✨ How do you currently find out when a state tax authority announces an extension or policy change?
- Think of a recent state announcement — walk me through what you did when you heard about it.
- ✨ Has there been a time you found out late — from a client, a group chat, a news feed? What happened?
- How often do you proactively scan state DOR websites vs. wait for things to reach you?
- When LA County got disaster-extended last year, did it affect any of your clients? How did you handle it?

### Story 2 — tool history (conversion blocker)
- What have you tried before? Walk me through each one.
- ✨ Why did you stop using [TaxDome / Karbon / Drake / other]?
- How long did the setup take? Did you finish setting it up?
- What was the specific moment you decided "this isn't for me"?
- What would a tool have to prove in the first 10 minutes for you to keep using it?

### Wildcards — let them steer
- What's something in your work that surprises people outside accounting?
- Is there a client type or state that gives you disproportionate anxiety? Why?
- If I could automate exactly one thing in your week, what would it be?
- What question should I be asking that I haven't?

## 6 · 5 rules that matter more than any question

1. **Shut up for 60% of the call.** After you ask a question, count to 5 in your head before speaking. The real answer comes in the second wave.
2. **Never ask "what are your pain points?"** You'll get abstract nothings. Ask about *specific recent moments*: "Walk me through the last time…" "Tell me about the morning when…"
3. **Don't describe the product.** If they ask what you're building, keep it to one sentence and redirect. *"I'm earlier than that — I'm talking with people first."*
4. **Invalidate, don't validate.** Listen for things that contradict your assumptions. If someone says *"honestly TaxDome works fine for me"* — that's the most valuable data you'll collect today.
5. **Record or take dense notes with exact phrases.** "I spend more time figuring out what to do than doing it" is gold. Your paraphrase of it is bronze.

## 8 · Synthesis across interviews (after every 3 calls)

Don't wait until all interviews are done. Every 3 calls, do this 20-min pass:

1. **Open all 3 synthesis docs side-by-side.**
2. **List patterns** — anything said by 2+ CPAs. These are signals.
3. **List contradictions** — where CPAs disagree. These are segmentation clues ("solo vs. small-firm differ on X").
4. **List surprises** — things nobody in your team predicted. These often become product insights.
5. **Update your strategy docs** — specifically [strategy-03-customer-journey.md](./strategy-03-customer-journey.md) and [strategy-01-positioning.md](./strategy-01-positioning.md). If the journey you wrote doesn't match what CPAs describe, the journey is wrong.

## 9 · How many interviews is enough?

| Count | What you learn |
|-------|----------------|
| 3 | Basic vocabulary, surface pains. Don't stop here. |
| 5 | Repeated patterns emerge. You start hearing the same phrases. |
| 7–8 | Segmentation clarity (solo vs. small firm, generalist vs. compliance-heavy). |
| 10+ | Diminishing returns *unless* you expand to a new segment. |

**Rule of thumb:** when the 8th interview teaches you nothing new, stop interviewing and start prototyping. Go back to interviews only if a major assumption shifts.

## 10 · Decision gates (what these interviews should conclude)

After 5–8 calls, you should be able to answer:

- [ ] **Is Story 1 real?** Do CPAs describe the weekly triage pain unprompted, or only when I ask?
- [ ] **Is Story 3 real?** Do 3+ CPAs have a specific story of finding out about a state announcement late?
- [ ] **Is Story 2 real?** Do 3+ CPAs describe abandoning a tool specifically during setup/migration?
- [ ] **Is my primary persona right?** Does Sarah's profile match who I'm talking to, or am I hearing a different archetype more often?
- [ ] **Is the pricing defensible?** Would ≥ 50% of interviewed CPAs pay $49/mo if the product delivered on Story 3?
- [ ] **What surprised me?** What emerged from interviews that I didn't predict, and does it change positioning?

Answer YES to 4+ → proceed to build.
Answer NO to 2+ → re-scope before building.
Mixed / unclear → run 3 more targeted interviews on the unclear question.
