# DueDateHQ — Positioning

> The external framing. Who it's for, what category, why it's different. Uses April Dunford's framework enriched with a Geoffrey Moore positioning statement and a Neumeier Onliness Test.

---

## 1. The Moore-style positioning statement (paste-ready)

> **For** solo and small-firm CPAs (1–5 people, 20–300 clients, multi-state practice)
> **Who** are drowning in Excel deadline sheets and still find out about state extensions from their clients,
> **DueDateHQ is** a cloud-native state-deadline intelligence layer
> **That** monitors 50+ state tax authorities around the clock and shows affected clients within 24 hours of any announcement.
> **Unlike** File In Time (desktop, manual updates) or Karbon/Canopy (practice-management platforms priced for 10+ person firms),
> **DueDateHQ** is priced for a solo practitioner ($29–$49/mo), ships with a pre-built 50-state database, and auto-detects which of *your* clients are affected when a state authority makes an announcement.

## 2. April Dunford breakdown

| Element | DueDateHQ |
|---|---|
| **Competitive alternatives** | Excel + Outlook (default); File In Time (direct); Karbon / TaxDome / Canopy (adjacent practice management) |
| **Unique attributes** | (a) Auto-monitored 50-state deadline database; (b) 24h state-announcement SLA with affected-client matching; (c) Solo-friendly pricing with no annual lock-in |
| **Value (and proof)** | (a) 45 → 5 min weekly triage; (b) Never be the last CPA in your state to know about an extension; (c) Penalty liability backstop via accuracy SLA |
| **Target best-fit customer** | Solo or 2–5 person CPA/EA firm, 20–300 clients, multi-state, currently using Excel + Outlook or File In Time |
| **Market category** | "State-deadline intelligence for CPAs" — a deliberately narrow sub-category of tax workflow software. Not practice management. Not a client portal. |

### Why this category frame matters

Competing as "tax practice management" means we lose to Karbon and Canopy on breadth. Competing as "tax deadline tracker" commoditizes us against File In Time. **"State-deadline intelligence layer"** is a category of one — and categories of one are defensible.

## 4. What we are explicitly NOT

Positioning is as much about rejection as assertion. We are not:

- ❌ A practice-management platform (that's Karbon)
- ❌ A client portal (that's TaxDome)
- ❌ Tax resolution software (that's Canopy)
- ❌ Bookkeeping software (that's QuickBooks)
- ❌ Tax preparation software (that's Drake / ProConnect / UltraTax)
- ❌ A billing / invoicing tool
- ❌ A document vault
- ❌ A desktop installer — ever (that's File In Time's trap)

**Signal to the market:** we are the *specialist layer* that sits alongside your existing stack. We answer exactly one question excellently — *"what's due for whom this week, and did anything change over the weekend?"*
