## Delivery tips (universal)

- **The 60-second rule.** You earn each additional minute. The Louisiana example must land in the first 90 seconds.
- **Invite conversation.** Pause after the Lacerte-organizer test. Pause after each verbatim quote in §3. **If a listener starts talking, let them.** They are selling themselves on the idea. The more they talk, the more they convince themselves.
- **Own the customer's voice.** Verbatim quotes (especially bilingual) are stickier than your own paraphrase. Memorize three: the supply-side quote, the wedge quote, the timing quote. Use them in that order.
- **Own the numbers.** "~46K US firms, ~45K ICP, blended $60/mo ARPU" should roll off without looking. If you can't, drop the number; don't fake it.
- **Handle "I don't know" well.** *"I don't know yet, but here's how I'd find out"* beats a fabricated answer. The CAC question is the most likely one for which "I don't know yet" is the right answer.
- **The 2-sentence opener is everything.** Practice until effortless. Test on three smart non-CPA friends — if they can't paraphrase it back, simplify further.
