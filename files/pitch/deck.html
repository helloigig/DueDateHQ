Failure mode 1
Monday-morning anxiety
She reopens her Excel sheet and tries to remember what was urgent before the weekend. Every Monday, reconstructed from short-term memory.
Failure mode 2
The chase loop
Three weeks before any deadline, she hand-types the same reminder email twelve times. Some clients respond. Many don't. One inevitably surprises her with a 1099 she didn't ask for.
Failure mode 3
The state-change miss
When Louisiana changes an estimated payment rule mid-year, she finds out from a client's complaint, not from her tools.
She'll pay $49/mo to make all three go away. She will not switch to TaxDome and re-learn her stack.
